About

Wholesale, from our family to your kitchen ഞങ്ങളുടെ കുടുംബത്തിൽ നിന്ന് നിങ്ങളുടെ അടുക്കളയിലേക്ക് മൊത്തവ്യാപാരം

Mancherikalam Big Bazaar is our wholesale shop in Karukachal, supplying caterers, hotels, hostels, canteens and event organisers across Kottayam district. We carry the staples in bulk pack sizes — sacks of rice, tins of oil, catering-quantity spices and disposables — at wholesale rates. Tell us what you need and by when, and we will quote you on WhatsApp.
