About us

A family store that stocks like a warehouse ഗോഡൗൺ പോലെ സ്റ്റോക്കുള്ള ഒരു കുടുംബ സ്ഥാപനം

MancherikalaM BigBazar is our family's wholesale and retail store in Changanassery. Walk in for a week's groceries, or send us a list for a wedding feast — our warehouse holds rice, oils, flour, sugar and spices in bulk, and the shop floor carries the everyday brands Kerala kitchens rely on. Tell us what you need and by when, and we will quote you on WhatsApp.
